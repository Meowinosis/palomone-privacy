Palomone Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Summary

Palomone, published by firewhizcorp, does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. The app works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, statistics (solve times, streaks) and settings are saved only on your device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

No accounts, ads or tracking

Palomone has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking SDKs. It makes no network requests while you play.

Children

Because no personal data is collected from anyone, Palomone collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
